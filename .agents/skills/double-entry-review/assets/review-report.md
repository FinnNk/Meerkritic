## Findings

For each: stable ID; category (bug, design, boundary, test evidence, hypothesis); severity;
confidence; reviewed commit and file/lines; governing obligation; observed evidence;
consequence; suggested validation. Distinguish verified facts from suspected issues.

## Checks and limitations

assumptions challenged; relevant diary/blame/caller evidence; test-retirement concerns.

## Remaining review and next gate

Record a SHA-bound cursor only when the review step really completed.
